# Regimes

The same block asks different questions depending on whether the product exists. The regime is fixed at intake and recorded in the state header. It sets the evidence bar for every block.

---

## Greenfield

A product that does not exist yet, in a category the company has not sold into.

**Evidence bar.** Hypotheses are acceptable and must be labeled as hypotheses. The canvas is a set of claims with tests attached, not a description.

**What changes.** `P1` cannot cite the company's own customer evidence, so it must cite someone's — interviews, market research, competitor behavior, or the operator's direct experience, named as such. A `P1` that cites nothing is a `P1` about the founder's intuition, which is legitimate but must be labeled.

**Characteristic failure.** Writing the whole canvas in the indicative mood. Every block reads as fact, none of it has been tested, and the launch discovers all of it at once.

**Enforcement.** Every block's assumptions section is mandatory and typically the longest part of the block. If a greenfield block produces fewer than three falsifiable assumptions, it was written as description rather than as hypothesis.

---

## Portfolio extension

A new product for a company that already sells to some version of this customer.

**Evidence bar.** Anything about the existing customer base must come from the base, not from the market. The company already has the data; using external benchmarks where internal evidence exists is a research failure, not a shortcut.

**What changes.** `P3` and `P4` start from who is already bought from and ask how far the new product moves from that. `P6` becomes one of the most consequential blocks in the canvas rather than a formality — most extension failures are dependency failures. `M5` and `V1` should inherit channel and qualification learning from existing products, and the block should say explicitly what it inherited and what it deliberately broke from.

**Characteristic failure.** Assuming the existing customer wants the new product because they are the existing customer. The test is whether they have a budget line for it, which is a `P5` question, not a `P3` one.

**Enforcement.** Every block that could be answered from internal data and was not must say so. "No internal data was consulted for this section" is a valid line and an uncomfortable one, which is the point.

---

## Repositioning

An existing product being re-aimed: new segment, new category, new price, or new story.

**Evidence bar.** Hypotheses about how the product behaves are not acceptable. The product has customers, usage, churn, and lost deals. The canvas is written against that record.

**What changes.** The canvas is partly archaeological. `P1` must distinguish the problem the product was built for from the problem it is actually bought for — those diverge, and the divergence is usually the reason repositioning is on the table. `P2` should be written twice: what is promised today and what will be promised. `P5` inherits a price and a customer base that will react to changing it. `C2` and `C8` have real churn data and must use it.

**Characteristic failure.** Repositioning the message without repositioning the product, then measuring the failure as a marketing problem. `P2` and `M2` will contradict each other under check if this is happening; that contradiction is the whole finding.

**Enforcement.** Every block asks first what the current answer is, before proposing the new one. A repositioning canvas with no "today" column is a greenfield canvas wearing a costume.

---

## Applying the regime

At the start of each block, take one line to state what the regime demands of that block. Not a disclaimer — a constraint. For example, in `P3` under repositioning: the ICP must be derived from who currently retains and expands, not from who the company wishes bought it.

When the operator's answers stop matching the declared regime — a repositioning operator who cannot produce any usage data, an extension operator who has never sold to the segment — say so and propose changing the regime. Running the wrong regime produces a canvas that is internally consistent and externally wrong.
